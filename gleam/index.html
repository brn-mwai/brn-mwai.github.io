See it work

Gleam in ninety seconds

A real walk through the app: asking Gleam for help on screen, a room of experts talking an idea through, and the pointers that work together on the same page.

What it does

Help that comes out of the box

Every other assistant answers in a chat box and leaves you to find the button. Gleam comes onto your screen and points at it.

Shows you, on your screen

Ask how to do something in any app. Gleam looks at your screen, zooms in on the exact control, circles it in pencil, explains it, and clicks for you when you ask.

A room of experts you build

Lawyers, engineers, designers or your own team: any expert you like, with a name, craft, personality, face, voice and skills. They talk your idea through and answer each other by name.

A different brain for each

Claude Fable 5.1, Opus, Sonnet or Haiku, GPT-5, Llama or GPT-OSS on Groq. Put a Claude and a Llama at the same table and hear them genuinely disagree.

Pointers that work together

Say "all of you, go to this site and explain it". Every expert's pointer comes onto your desktop, one opens the page and the others point, draw and explain their part in turn.

Just talk

Hold Right Ctrl and speak. Your words appear live as you talk, and whoever you asked answers "On it" in their own voice before the full reply is even ready.

A shared canvas

Experts write their best points in handwriting and sketch wireframes in pencil while they talk. Pin your own notes, ask the room about any of them, and copy the board out as text.

Memory and a knowledge graph

Each expert remembers what you tell them about yourself and builds a graph of facts about your work, from your chats and from notes you give them.

Waits for your OK

Anything that sends, posts, deletes, buys or installs is held until you press Allow. Every action Gleam takes is written to a log on your computer.

Routines and teach by showing

Have a room check in every weekday at nine. Or do a task once while Gleam watches, and it writes a skill your expert can follow next time. What you type is never recorded.

Inside Gleam

Built for real work, not a demo

The cartoon boardroom won the room over on build day. Gleam's real job is your own team of experts, on your own problems.

A meeting that actually meets

Pitch an idea and the room takes turns. Experts build on each other, push back by name, raise a hand when they need something from you, and Gleam chairs: it frames the question, keeps order and says back who owns what.

  • Say "all of you" and everyone answers once, in order
  • Name one expert and only they reply
  • Run the meeting from framing to decision, then print the minutes

Everyone on your screen at once

Call your experts and their pointers stand on your desktop together, each in their own colour. The one speaking steps forward. When one takes the screen, the rest wait their turn, because Windows has one mouse.

  • Works across any app: browsers, Figma, spreadsheets, email
  • Zooms in for a sharp look before it points or clicks
  • Pointers go home on their own when the room goes quiet

Every expert, their own mind

Pick the provider and the model for each expert. Give them skills as SKILL.md files, teach them facts from your notes, and they keep what they learn about you between conversations.

  • Claude on your subscription through Claude Code, or on your API key
  • Groq for the fastest replies and live captions
  • Memory you can read, edit and clear at any time

How it works

Three minutes from download to your first room

No account, no sign-up, no server of ours. Gleam runs on your computer with your own keys.

Install

Download the installer and run it. It installs for your Windows user only, with no admin rights needed.

Follow the welcome

Tell Gleam your name, connect a brain (your Claude subscription or a key) and, if you like, a voice. Every key is tested before it is kept. Then pick a first team of experts.

Talk to it

Hold Right Ctrl and ask. Ctrl + Shift wakes Gleam beside your cursor, Shift + drag circles anything to ask about it, and Alt + G shows every shortcut.

A welcome that sets everything up

New users are walked through six short steps. Gleam finds the Claude Code CLI by itself, tests each key against its service, checks your microphone, and creates a first room of original experts on the best brain you connected.

Privacy and safety

Your computer, your keys, your data

We run no servers and collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking in the app or on this site.

Keys sealed by Windows

API keys are encrypted with Windows DPAPI so only your Windows user can read them. They never leave your computer except to go to the service they belong to.

Straight to the services you choose

When you ask something, your words, and a screenshot when Gleam needs to look, go directly from your computer to the provider you connected. Nothing passes through us.

You approve risky actions

Screen actions that send, post, delete, buy, install or deploy wait for your OK. Gleam only acts on requests you start, and logs every action on your computer.

Listening only when you ask

The microphone opens while you hold Right Ctrl or turn on Always-on. Audio is not kept. Teach by showing counts keystrokes but never records what you type.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Gleam free?

Yes, Gleam itself is free to install and use. The AI and voice services it connects to are paid for by you, directly, under your own accounts: your Claude plan or API usage, and optionally Groq, OpenAI, NVIDIA and Fish Audio.

Does Gleam send my data anywhere?

Only to the services you connect, and only when you use a feature that needs them. We run no servers and receive nothing. Your rooms, notes, memory and settings stay in your Windows user folder. See the Privacy Policy.

Can Gleam do things on my computer without asking?

Gleam only acts on requests you start. Anything that sends, posts, deletes, buys, installs or deploys waits for you to press Allow, and every action is written to a log you can read. Press Esc to stop Gleam at any time.

Which brain should I use?

If you have a Claude subscription, Claude Code on your plan is the simplest. An Anthropic API key unlocks Claude Fable 5.1 for the sharpest screen work. Add a Groq key for the fastest hearing and replies.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Gleam controls the desktop through Windows, so it runs on Windows 10 and 11 for now.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

Gleam is new and not yet code-signed, so Windows has not seen it before. Check the SHA256 above matches, then choose More info and Run anyway. Signing is next on the list.

Is Gleam open source?

No. Gleam is proprietary software by Brian Mwai. You are free to install and run it; the source code, name, mascot and logo are not licensed for reuse. See the Terms of Use.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, find Gleam and choose Uninstall. To remove your rooms, keys and settings too, delete the Gleam folder in %APPDATA%.
